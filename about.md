I'm a fourth-year Mechanical Engineering major and Computer Science Minor at the University of Florida (UF). Ever since I was a child, I've been invovled in the Vietnamese Eucharistic Youth Movement, a Catholic youth organization. I became a youth leader in 2024 where I help teach lessons about scripture and specialized skills, as well as various community involvements such as volunteering. After COVID-19 restrictions began to lift my junior year of highschool, I joined the cologuard team and continued to perform with them until I graduated, serving as colorguard captain my senior year. 

After graduating, I began teaching colorguard at Santa Fe Highschool. Helping teach colorguard technique and performance skills to highschool students, the team ended the season in third place at state championships, the best outcome given the limited resources available at the school. The following year, I was elected as Performance Director for the Vietnamese Student Organization (VSO). This role invovled immense planning prior to major performances, notably the Asian American Student Assembly (AASA), Tet Trung Thu (Vietnamese Mid-Autumn Festival), and Tet (Vietnamese New Years). It required the selection and coordination of student choreographers, coordination of 100+ dancers, coordinating with other performance directors from other organizations to minimize practice conflicts, and other duties required of VSO officers for the functioning of the organization as a whole. Other minor responsibilities included making graphics for the VSO Dance Instagram, planning choreographer photoshoots for choreographer reveals on the instagram, planning dance workshops, coordinating with Ace Dance Company for collaboration workshops.
